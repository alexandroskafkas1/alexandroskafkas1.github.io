Course Overview & Materials

Calculus of functions in two or more dimensions. Includes solid analytic geometry, partial differentiation, multiple integration, and selected topics in vector calculus.

  • Lecture/Credit: 4 hours / 4 hours
  • Prerequisites: MATH 142 Calculus II or MATH 148 Honors Calculus II
  • Textbook: Calculus: Early Transcendentals, 9th ed., by Stewart, Clegg, and Watson (Accessed via Canvas / VolBooks Total Access program at no extra cost).
  • Calculator Policy: Calculators may not be used on tests or quizzes, but may be used to assist with homework.

Assignments & Assessments

Homework: Assigned in WebAssign (linked via Canvas). Due at 11:59 PM exactly one week after the lesson is covered. The three lowest homework scores are dropped at the end of the semester.

Quizzes: Eleven short quizzes (2 problems each) given at the end of most Tuesdays. The two lowest quiz scores are dropped to offset emergencies.

Tests & Exams:

  • Midterm 1: Covers Lessons 1–14 (Chs. 12.2–6, 13, 14.1–6)
  • Midterm 2: Covers Lessons 15–26 (Chs. 14.7–8, 15)
  • Final Exam: Comprehensive exam held during the official university exam window (December 3–9, 2026). Exact section times will follow UTK's official exam schedule.
